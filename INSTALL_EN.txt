Model Pack + ReCurve + Fast Draft 0.1.152-beta
================================

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a permanent ASCII folder, for example:
   C:/MayaTools/ModelPack_ReCurve
2. Open Maya and choose a Shelf tab.
3. Drag install_model_pack.py from that folder into a Maya 3D viewport.
4. Save the shelf: Shelf > Save All Shelves.

Keep all package files together. Reinstall after moving the folder.
The existing MP shelf button is updated. ReCurve is included and loads
automatically; a separate ReCurve installation is not required.

Select edge chains and click ReSegments or Control Curve in their grid row.
Their controls, Apply and Cancel expand directly underneath.

Shortcuts: Shift+R, Shift+Wheel, Ctrl+Shift+Wheel, Alt, Space/Enter and Esc.
Curve deformation updates while dragging. See README.md for full instructions
and VALIDATION.md for the tested environments and coverage.

Click Fast Draft for the embedded Cylinder / Cube choice. Drag a footprint on a
visible mesh, release, then drag outward from the surface to add or inward to cut.
Depth follows the surface normal's screen direction. A face-on normal uses a
stable fallback: up adds, down cuts. Release to commit.
Shift+Wheel adjusts cylinder sides in place. Cubes have no subdivisions or Shift controls.
Esc cancels the sketch; Q exits. Fast Draft is bundled and requires no installer
or plug-in of its own. The outline is explicitly turquoise (#00CCCC).

Added cylinders have soft side edges, hard cap borders and editable Radius,
Height, Sides and Top Cap in the Channel Box. With Model Pack open, select one
and press T in the viewport: Cap / Center / Parallel. This changes the top cap
through persistent history, with Undo/Redo and UVs retained. Other objects keep
their normal Maya T behavior. Cylinder cutters use N-gon caps and smooth walls,
without the unwanted central floor vertex.

Shift+Wheel requires active ReSegments. Ctrl+Z undoes live segment steps;
Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redoes them. History does not need to be enabled or deleted.
Settings grow vertically and keep the window width fixed while switching.

Script Editor alternative (Python tab; replace the folder path):

    import runpy
    runpy.run_path(r'C:/MayaTools/ModelPack_ReCurve/install_model_pack.py', run_name='__main__')
